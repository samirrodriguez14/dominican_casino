Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

No real-money gambling. Dominican Casino uses virtual chips and scores for match play only. There is no wagering with real currency and no cash-out.

What we collect

  • Firebase Authentication identifier — an anonymous user ID when you first open the app.
  • Display name — the name you choose for your profile and at the table.
  • Linked account details — if you connect Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email address from that provider and link them to your Firebase account.
  • Push notification token (FCM) — only if you allow notifications; stored under your user profile, not on shared game documents.
  • Match data — games you create or join, including table state, scores, and player participation needed for realtime play.
  • Virtual economy — coins and energy balances stored on your user document at users/{uid} in Firebase.

How we use data

Data is processed with Firebase (Google Cloud) to run multiplayer games, sync tables in realtime, deliver turn alerts, and maintain your profile and virtual balances. We do not sell your personal information.

Tracking

We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Analytics are limited to what Firebase provides for app operation and crash reporting.

Your choices

  • You can adjust notification permission in iOS Settings at any time.
  • You can clear local profile preferences from Settings in the app.
  • Account deletion — from Settings, you can permanently delete your Firebase account and associated profile data. Match history on completed tables may remain in anonymized form where other players participated.
